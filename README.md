
# Expense Tracker App - Project Outline

## I. Project Description

A. The Expense Tracker is a simple app that helps users keep track of their expenses.

B. Users will be able to enter expenses and view them in the app.

C. The app will show the user how much they have spent.

## II. Problem Being Addressed

A. It can be easy to forget how much money you spend.

B. The app will give users a simple way to keep track of their expenses.

C. Having expenses in one place can make them easier to review.

## III. Platform

A. The app will be designed for mobile devices.

B. The app will be developed for Android.

## IV. Front-End and Back-End Support

### A. Front-End

The front end will include the screens and buttons users interact with.

Users will be able to enter and view their expenses.

### B. Back-End

The back end will handle the information entered into the app.

It will keep track of the user's expenses and total spending.

## V. Functionality

A. Users can enter an expense.

B. Users can view their expenses.

C. Users can see their total spending.

D. Users can remove an expense if needed.

## VI. Design and Wireframes

A. The app will have a simple design.

B. The main screen will display the user's expenses and total spending.

C. A separate screen or section will allow users to enter a new expense.

D. Basic wireframes will be created to show what the app will look like.

## VII. Project Goals

A. Create a simple app that helps users keep track of their expenses.

B. Make the app easy to use and understand.

C. Allow users to quickly enter and review their expenses.

D. Complete a working version of the app by the end of the term.
  

## Project Progress

So far, I have completed my project outline and created wireframes showing how I want the Expense Tracker app to look. I have planned the main features, including adding expenses, viewing spending totals, and organizing expenses into categories. I also plan to include a monthly budget feature to help users manage their spending.

The next step is to begin building the app in Android Studio. Once the main features are working, I will test the app, fix any issues, and upload the code to this repository.
